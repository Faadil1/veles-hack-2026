# What HYPER-AI is
HYPER-AI is an EU-funded Horizon Europe project. It works on distributed computing swarms: autonomous networks of interconnected nodes across Cloud, Edge and IoT that adapt and optimise resources across the computing continuum, aiming at self-configuring, self-healing and self-optimising behaviour. Its broader ambition is to make complex Cloud, Edge and IoT environments easier to manage through intelligent, autonomous technologies.

# Hyperion
Hyperion is the LLM-powered agentic assistant for the HYPER-AI IDE. It is a microservice that lets users interact with the IDE in natural language: it answers questions about HYPER-AI and acts in the IDE on the user's behalf, including creating, editing and deleting files. Relevant techniques: large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, Server-Sent Events, session memory, Python and Docker.

# VelesHack 2026
HYPER-AI co-organises VelesHack 2026 ("Riding the Edge-to-Cloud Continuum") with CoGNETs, ENACT and O-CEI, hosted by Universitat Politècnica de València on 6–8 October 2026 with remote participation. Each of the four challenges has its own winner and runner-up.
